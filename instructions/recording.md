# Recording tips (tell the user before they record)

Keep this short when relaying it: three or four bullets.

- Plan the story first: which 4–8 steps should the viewer see?
- Move the mouse calmly and pause about 2 seconds after each click.
- Use demo data. Avoid real customer names, emails or phone numbers on screen.
- Don't worry about mistakes or waiting. Idle time and slips can be cut afterwards.
- Anything you save or submit while recording really happens in the app.
- Close the window to finish.
